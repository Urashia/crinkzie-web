TERMS OF SERVICE

Last updated: October 2026

1. About Crinkzie

Crinkzie is a small, student-run business that sells freshly baked crinkles. It is operated by one person and is intended only for students of the University of Caloocan City (UCC), Congressional Campus.

2. Who Can Order

Crinkzie is available to UCC Congressional Campus students only. By creating an account, you confirm that you are a current student of this campus and that the information you give (name, year and section, student ID, contact number) is accurate.

3. Products and Online Ordering

The website allows users to:

  • Browse available crinkles
  • Add products to a cart
  • Place orders
  • Pay through the available payment methods
  • Monitor order status

All products are sold as listed. Available products, prices, ordering periods, and stock quantities are set by Crinkzie and may change at any time.

4. Freshly Baked, No Cancellations

Every Crinkzie order is baked fresh after it is placed. Because of this, orders cannot be cancelled, changed, or refunded once they have been placed. Please review your cart carefully before checking out.

5. Pre-Orders and Ready Stock

Crinkzie uses both pre-orders and limited ready stock. For ready-stock orders, the system checks available inventory before confirming your quantity. For pre-orders, your order creates a baking requirement, and items are baked for the scheduled batch.

6. Payment

Crinkzie accepts the following payment methods:

  • QR-based payment: Your payment must be verified before your order is baked. Verification may take some time, and Crinkzie may hold or decline an order if payment cannot be confirmed.
  • Cash on campus: You pay in full in cash when you receive your order within the UCC Congressional Campus. Please prepare the exact amount when possible.

Because every order is baked fresh and cannot be cancelled, customers who repeatedly fail to claim or pay for cash orders may be restricted from placing future orders.

7. Campus-Only Delivery

All orders are delivered within the UCC Congressional Campus only. Crinkzie cannot cater to or deliver outside the campus, and no exceptions can be made. Customers are responsible for providing accurate details and being available during the scheduled delivery or handoff time. Orders that are not received may not be replaced or refunded.

8. Accounts and Data Access

Customers can only view their own account information and order history. You are responsible for keeping your password secure and for all activity under your account.

9. Privacy and Data Retention

Crinkzie collects only the information needed to process your orders. Your personal information and order records are kept private and are not sold or shared with third parties. All customer data will be deleted at the end of the semester.

Crinkzie products do not contain nuts, but they do contain common baking ingredients such as flour, eggs, and milk. Customers with allergies should check before ordering.
