About Us

Crinkzie started with a simple idea: bake a great treat that isn't complicated, but still feels special. We focused on what makes a crinkle worth craving — rich chocolate, a soft and fudgy center, and that signature chewy bite. Together, they create a treat you'll want to reach for again.
